            Combat rules

            All counters advance together at each participant’s Speed, pausing at 1,000 for a turn. The acting counter spends 1,000; other counters keep their progress. Ties use roster order. Each turn grants 5 base AP before class modifiers. Zero Speed does not advance.

            Weapon attacks pit the attacker’s Accuracy against the target’s Evasion: hit chance = Accuracy ÷ (Accuracy + Evasion). Ranged attacks face only half of the target’s Evasion. Heavy armor lowers Evasion, light armor lowers it a little and clothing raises it. After a hit lands, Crit Chance is rolled separately. Crit Rating adds up the weapon type's base (daggers and crossbows high, heavy weapons low), 0.15 × Luck (another 0.1 × Luck with a weapon that scales with Luck) and every class, artifact, weapon-part and rune bonus. Rating becomes Crit Chance on a smooth curve with no hard cap: 10 rating ≈ 10%, 50 ≈ 39%, 100 ≈ 63%, 150 ≈ 78%; only skills guarantee a crit. Crit momentum: every landed hit that does not crit makes the next one more likely and a crit resets it, so crits arrive steadily at the shown rate. Critical hits deal 25% more damage; class bonuses and the weapon type's own bonus (heavy weapons hit hardest) add to that, up to a total of 75% more. Crits do not pierce Armor by themselves; explicit Armor Pierce effects still work. Resistance applies first (worn armor adds elemental resistances), then damage × 100 / (100 + 2 × Armor), rounded down with a minimum of 1 for a positive, non-immune hit. Enemies without worn armor have natural armor: 20, thicker on young beasts around level 8. Spells always hit and ignore Armor; only elemental resistance reduces them.

            Guard and Evade replace each other and expire at the start of your next turn. Spell and skill durations count the affected participant’s turns. Meditate restores 20% of maximum Mana + 0.5 × Mind + 0.2 × Luck, rounded down. Run chance is 50 + 0.5 × Speed + 0.2 × Luck − 0.4 × fastest living enemy Speed. Switching to the sheet pauses enemy actions.
