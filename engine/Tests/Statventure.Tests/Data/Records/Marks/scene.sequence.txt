scene.sequence "Cutscene" in world/cutscenes by PlainNames
  help: A cutscene: tracks on a timeline that move the stage and the figures, cut between cameras, light the scene, start particles, play sound and change the screen.
  name "Name"
    help: The cutscene's name, used in the messages about it.
  duration "Duration": unit s; limits above 0
    help: How long the cutscene lasts. When it ends, or the player skips it, every track is as it is at this time.
  skippable "Skippable"
    help: Whether the player can skip the cutscene.
  blocking "Blocking"
    help: Whether the game holds the battle while the cutscene plays. Leave it off for a short scene that plays beside the battle.
  roles "Roles"
    help: The names the game fills in when it plays the cutscene: figures, scene nodes, models, screen elements and palettes.
  stages "Stages": references model in models/stages by PathNames
    help: The stages the cutscene brings, each a model at models/stages/<name>. The tracks call a stage by its name, the last part of its path.
  tracks "Tracks"
    help: What happens on the timeline. Where two tracks set the same thing, the later one wins.
  reduced "Reduced"
    help: A shorter version, played when the player's system asks for reduced motion: parts of the timeline in order, each played and then held (not set: none).
scene.role "Sequence role"
  help: A name the game fills in when it plays the cutscene, such as the boss's figure or the card on the screen.
  name "Name"
    help: The name the tracks use for it.
  kind "Kind"
    help: What the game puts there: a figure (actor), a scene node, a model, a screen element or a palette.
scene.track "Track"
  help: One kind of thing that happens on the timeline.
scene.span "Sequence span"
  help: A part of the timeline the shorter version plays, then holds at its end.
  from "From": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: Where the part starts on the timeline.
  to "To": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: Where the part ends on the timeline (the same as its start: only that moment, held).
  hold "Hold": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: How long the part's end is held after it plays.
scene.clip-track "Clip track"

  strip "Strip"

  locationY "Location Y": unit m

  locationZ "Location Z": unit m
    help: The node's location along Z over time.
  rotationX "Rotation X": unit rad

  rotationY "Rotation Y": unit rad
    help: The node's turn about Y over time.
  rotationZ "Rotation Z": unit rad
    help: The node's turn about Z over time.
  scaleX "Scale X"
    help: The node's scale along X over time (1: its size).
  scaleY "Scale Y"
    help: The node's scale along Y over time.
  scaleZ "Scale Z"
    help: The node's scale along Z over time.

scene.strip "Sequence strip"
  help: A clip placed on the timeline, as a strip in Blender's NLA editor.

    help: The clip's name, as it is called in its model.
  start "Start": unit s

  clipStart "Clip start": unit s; limits 0 or more

  clipEnd "Clip end": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: Where in the clip it stops playing (not set: the clip's end).
  scale "Scale": limits above 0
    help: How much longer the clip takes to play: 2 plays it at half speed.

  reversed "Reversed"
    help: Plays the clip backwards.
  blendIn "Blend in": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: How long the clip takes to fade in over what is below it.
  blendOut "Blend out": unit s; limits 0 or more
    help: How long the clip takes to fade out at its end.
  influence "Influence": limits 0 or more and at most 1
    help: How strongly the clip counts, from 0 (not at all) to 1 (fully; also when not set).
  mode "Mode"
    help: How the clip mixes with what is below it: replace it, or add to it.
  extrapolation "Extrapolation"
    help: What the clip shows outside its time: nothing, its first and last frames (hold), or its last frame until the next clip (hold forward).
scene.curve "F-curve"

  smoothing "Smoothing"
    help: How the worked-out handles of a run of keys are smoothed: each from its neighbours alone, or together so the motion has no jolts (Blender's default).
