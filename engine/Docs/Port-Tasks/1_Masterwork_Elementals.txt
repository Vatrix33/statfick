The Elementals formerly each had a unique item, which is currently replaced with a placeholder. That placeholder is a forged weapon, so that placeholder can become permanent. The former unique items will be remade into masterwork items:
-Plasma Blade, spell wand, This spell wand is fire-element based. Its unique property is that has a small beam of red laser coming out of its gem, making it visually look like a lasersword. In addition to being a spell wand, it also has the properties of a longsword. Its primary scaling is Intelligence and its secondary scaling is dexterity. It deals only Fire damage. Since its base item is a spell wand, it has no rune slots. It has a 5% chance to be dropped by any fire-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Hellmade;
-Tidepiercer, lance, This lance is shaped like a trident and uses a crossguard instead of a pommel. Attacks with this lance ignore 50% of the targets armor. This weapon always spawns with the rune to transform half its damage into water damage. It has a 5% chance to be dropped from any water-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Dwarven;
-First Comet, mace, Attacks with this weapon have a 25% chance to stun the enemy. This weapon always spawns with the rune to transform half its damage into earth damage. It has a 5% chance to be dropped from any earth-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Hearthforge;
-Peace and Balance, quarterstaff, While holding this weapon, your turn counter cannot be reduced and your speed cannot be decreased. This weapon always spawns with the rune to transform half its damage into wind damage. It has a 5% chance to be dropped from any wind-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Elven;
-Kugelblitz, shortbow, Attacks with this weapon deal 15% of the dealt damage to all other enemies and marks all damaged enemies for 1 turn. Marked enemies have 50% increased Mana cost for spells. This weapon always spawns with the rune to transform half its damage into lightning damage. It has a 5% chance to be dropped from any lightning-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Church of Talos;
-Avalanche, light armor, Each time you take damage, you gain 1 Avalanche counter. Once you have 7 counters, reduce the speed of all enemies by 30% for 3 turns. This weapon always spawns with the rune to gain resistance in ice damage. It has a 5% chance to be dropped from any ice-based elemental. Its manufactorer is Roundtable;
