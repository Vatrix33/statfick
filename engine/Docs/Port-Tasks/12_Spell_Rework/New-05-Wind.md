# New spells 5: Wind (task 12)

**Status: a draft for the user's review (SP2, SP3).** Wind's fourteen new spells: two in each of Tiers 1–5, one in
each of Tiers 6–9, among them **Wind's new summon** (SP48). They follow the approved system and curve
(`Spell-Augmentation.md`), the sample's style and the element statuses (`Element-Statuses.md`). Prices are the tier's
usual. [X] values are for the raw caster of the system draft (no classes or gear) at the level the tier unlocks and at
Level 100, for a Wisdom-first caster.

## What Wind gains

- **Support it lacked:** Zephyr Touch (Wind's only heal, with Speed), Fresh Breeze (blows away Soaked, Chilled and
  slows on the party), Gale Rush (every ally's next turn sooner), Calm Within (a guard against unbalancing, slowing
  and attacks), Windwalk (a quick, strong dodge).
- **Off-Balance, more ways:** Whistling Wind (no damage, every enemy), Air Blade (pays it off), Twister and Tornado
  (several rolls each), Whirlwind (sure on a group, a tier before Hurricane Choir), Gale Spear (2 AP at Tier 8), Storm
  Front (5 AP, every enemy).
- **Suffocate:** damage over time, Wind's own.
- **Summon - Storm Roc** (Tier 7): a new creature, flying, whose blows push enemies' turns back.

New mechanics: a cure for slows, choking damage at the target's next turns, a summon whose blows push turns back.

## The spells

### Zephyr Touch: Tier 1, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 12 Mana, 700 silver; power 5 + Wis ×0.28 + Speed ×0.08 (80% of the tier's reference). Heals [X] and raises their Speed by 10% for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Touch yourself or an ally with a gentle zephyr: [X] HP restored and Speed +10% for 2 turns."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 9; Level 100: 114.
- **Augments:** Power, Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Wind has no heal at all; a light one fits a support element of speed.

### Whistling Wind: Tier 1, offensive, all enemies (no damage) (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 6 Mana, 700 silver. Each enemy has a 30% chance to be put **Off-Balance** for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Send a whistling wind through the enemy line; each enemy has a 30% chance to be put Off-Balance for 2 turns."
- **Augments:** Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** The cheapest way to touch every enemy, like Ice's Minor Cold.

### Fresh Breeze: Tier 2, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver. Ends Soaked, Chilled and every Speed loss on every ally, and raises their Speed by 5% for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Let a fresh breeze through the party: every Soak, Chill and slow on them blows away, and their Speed rises by 5% for 2 turns."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** The cure for Water's and Ice's statuses in enemy hands, at a low tier.

### Air Blade: Tier 2, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver; power 8 + Wis ×0.46 + Speed ×0.14 (85% of the tier's reference). Deals 30% more to a target that is **Off-Balance**.
- **Description:** "Cut one enemy with a blade of air for [X] Wind damage, 30% more if it is Off-Balance."
- **[X]:** Level 10: 30; Level 100: 187.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Wind's single-target payoff for its own status.

### Twister: Tier 3, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.23 + Speed ×0.07 (30% of the tier's reference, per strike; three, 90% in all). Three strikes (new) at the target, each for [X] with a 20% chance to put it **Off-Balance** for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Catch one enemy in a twister that strikes it three times for [X] Wind damage, each with a 20% chance to put it Off-Balance."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 24; Level 100: 94 (each strike).
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Several hits on one target: each can unbalance, spread Burning or stack Basic's Exposed.

### Suffocate: Tier 3, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 6 + Wis ×0.39 + Speed ×0.11 (50% of the tier's reference, now; 30% of the reference again at each of its next 2 turns). The air is drawn from its lungs (new): at the start of each of its next 2 turns it takes 60% of [X] again.
- **Description:** "Draw the air from one enemy's lungs for [X] Wind damage; at the start of each of its next 2 turns it chokes for 60% of that again."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 39; Level 100: 157.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Wind's damage over time: more in all, for waiting.

### Gale Rush: Tier 4, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver. Every ally's next turn comes 25% sooner.
- **Description:** "Send a rushing gale behind the party: every ally's next turn comes 25% sooner."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Backwind for the whole party, without Skyfall Chorus's damage.

### Tornado: Tier 4, offensive, random enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.26 + Speed ×0.08 (28% of the tier's reference, per strike; four, 112% in all). Four strikes (new) at random enemies, each for [X] with a 25% chance to put its target **Off-Balance** for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Loose a tornado through the enemy line: four strikes at random enemies, each dealing [X] Wind damage with a 25% chance to put its target Off-Balance."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 36; Level 100: 106 (each strike).
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Many rolls on a group, and Wildfire spreads Burning with every hit on a burning enemy.

### Whirlwind: Tier 5, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 75 Mana, 8,800 silver; power 12 + Wis ×0.67 + Speed ×0.19 (55% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less its effects). Every enemy struck is always put **Off-Balance** for 2 turns, and its next turn comes 15% later.
- **Description:** "Sweep all enemies into a whirlwind for [X] Wind damage: every one of them is put Off-Balance and its next turn comes 15% later."
- **[X]:** Level 40: 120; Level 100: 272.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A sure group status a tier before Hurricane Choir, with Wind's hold on time.

### Windwalk: Tier 5, defensive, yourself (new spell)

- **The spell:** 2 AP, 52 Mana, 8,800 silver. Evasion +50% for 2 turns, and your next turn comes 20% sooner.
- **Description:** "Walk on the wind: your Evasion rises by 50% for 2 turns and your next turn comes 20% sooner. This spell costs 2 AP."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A quick, strong dodge for a caster under attack.

### Calm Within: Tier 6, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 100 Mana, 11,700 silver. For 3 turns, enemy attacks against them have 30% less Accuracy, and they can't be put Off-Balance or slowed.
- **Description:** "Give yourself or an ally the calm within the storm for 3 turns: enemy attacks against them lose 30% Accuracy, and nothing can unbalance or slow them."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A guard for one hero against Wind, Water and Ice in enemy hands.

### Summon - Storm Roc: Tier 7, summon (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 140 Mana, 17,500 silver; power 31 + Wis ×1.82 + Speed ×0.52 (100% of the tier's reference, as a summon). Summons a Storm Roc (a new creature for the port: Level 50, built like the game's Level 50 beasts, about 600 HP); its maximum HP rises by 200% of [X] and every attack it makes by 25% of [X] (SP29). It flies (Evasion +30%); each of its hits pushes the target's next turn 15% later and has a 40% chance to put it **Off-Balance** for 2 turns. One at a time.
- **Description:** "Summon a friendly Storm Roc whose maximum HP rises by twice [X] and whose attacks deal a quarter of [X] more; it flies, and each of its blows pushes its target's next turn back and has a 40% chance to put it Off-Balance. Only one may be active at a time."
- **[X]:** Level 60: 462; Level 100: 737.
- **Augments:** Mana cost, Summon Vitality, Summon Might, Summon Speed.
- **Why:** The new Wind summon (SP48), a late one beside the Tier 5 Wind Elemental; its look and stats are made with the port's other new creatures.

### Gale Spear: Tier 8, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 2 AP, 126 Mana, 25,000 silver; power 23 + Wis ×1.37 + Speed ×0.39 (65% of the tier's reference, the 2 AP rule). A 2 AP spell; it always puts the target **Off-Balance** for 2 turns and pushes its next turn 20% later.
- **Description:** "Hurl a spear of gale at one enemy for [X] Wind damage, putting it Off-Balance and pushing its next turn 20% later. This spell costs 2 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 70: 399; Level 100: 555.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A fast high-tier spell beside Tempest Needle (Tier 7).

### Storm Front: Tier 9, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 5 AP, 350 Mana, 35,000 silver; power 54 + Wis ×3.14 + Speed ×0.9 (120% of the tier's reference, the 5 AP rule on an all-enemies spell). Takes your whole turn (5 AP); every enemy struck is always put **Off-Balance** for 2 turns and its next turn comes 30% later.
- **Description:** "Pour your whole turn into a storm front over all enemies for [X] Wind damage: every one is put Off-Balance and its next turn comes 30% later. This spell costs 5 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 80: 1,035; Level 100: 1,273.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Wind's biggest group spell below Tier 10, for a whole turn.
