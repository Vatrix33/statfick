# New spells 3: Water (task 12)

**Status: a draft for the user's review (SP2, SP3).** Water's fourteen new spells: two in each of Tiers 1–5, one in
each of Tiers 6–9. They follow the approved system and curve (`Spell-Augmentation.md`), the sample's style and the
element statuses (`Element-Statuses.md`). Prices are the tier's usual. [X] values are for the raw caster of the system
draft (no classes or gear) at the level the tier unlocks and at Level 100, for a Wisdom-first caster.
**Updated 2026-11-12 (SP60, SP61):** some spells here borrow another element's status; each says so ("borrowed, SP60" or "SP61").

## What Water gains

- **Early help for the party:** Healing Mist (a party heal at Tier 1; Water's first today is at Tier 7), Purifying
  Spring (a party cleanse at Tier 3), Bubble Shield (a barrier that keeps Burning off).
- **Soaking, early and sure:** Splash (2 AP), Riptide (a sure Soak at Tier 2), Swirling Eddy (a sure group Soak at
  Tier 4), with Tidal Lash and Drowning Depths paying a soaked field off.
- **Control:** Water Prison takes one enemy out of its next turn, but it takes half damage while held.
- **Mana and endurance:** Mana Tide (every other ally regains a little Mana), Wellspring (heals return Mana to their
  healer), Eternal Spring (HP and Mana each turn for the party).
- **Big moments:** Tsunami (a 4 AP wave on every enemy), Ocean's Embrace (a great heal whose overflow becomes a
  barrier).

New mechanics: a hold that stuns and shields its target, Mana for the rest of the party, Mana returned for healing, a
heal's overflow turned into a barrier, a strike that reaches every Soaked enemy.

## The spells

### Splash: Tier 1, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 2 AP, 8 Mana, 700 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.23 + Int ×0.07 (65% of the tier's reference, the 2 AP rule). A 30% chance to **Soak** the target for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Splash one enemy for [X] Water damage, with a 30% chance to Soak it for 2 turns. This spell costs 2 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 7; Level 100: 102.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Water's fast spell at Tier 1 (Seablade is the Tier 2 one).

### Healing Mist: Tier 1, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 14 Mana, 700 silver; power 3 + Wis ×0.16 + Int ×0.05 (45% of the tier's reference, per ally; 75% for all allies, less again for the low tier). Heals every ally for [X].
- **Description:** "Spread a healing mist over the party: every ally regains [X] HP."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 5; Level 100: 72.
- **Augments:** Power, Mana cost.
- **Why:** The game's first party heal, today at Tier 7 for Water.

### Bubble Shield: Tier 2, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver; power 8 + Wis ×0.46 + Int ×0.14 (85% of the tier's reference, as a barrier). A **barrier** of [X] for 3 turns; while it holds, they can't be set Burning.
- **Description:** "Wrap yourself or an ally in a bubble of water: a barrier that absorbs [X] damage within 3 turns, and no Burning while it holds."
- **[X]:** Level 10: 32; Level 100: 205.
- **Augments:** Power, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Water's own shield, which also stands against Fire.

### Riptide: Tier 2, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver; power 7 + Wis ×0.41 + Int ×0.12 (75% of the tier's reference). Always **Soaks** the target for 2 turns, and has a 30% chance to put it **Off-Balance** (Wind's status, borrowed, SP60) for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Drag one enemy into a riptide for [X] Water damage, Soaking it for 2 turns, with a 30% chance to drag it Off-Balance."
- **[X]:** Level 10: 28; Level 100: 181.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A cheap, sure Soak for a Lightning or Ice ally's next spell (Water's sure ones today start at Tier 5).

### Purifying Spring: Tier 3, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.23 + Int ×0.07 (30% of the tier's reference, per ally). Removes one debuff from every ally and heals each for [X].
- **Description:** "Raise a purifying spring among the party: every ally is cleansed of one debuff and regains [X] HP."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 25; Level 100: 102.
- **Augments:** Power, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Water's first party cleanse, ten tiers before Tideborn Refuge.

### Tidal Lash: Tier 3, offensive, one target and every Soaked enemy (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 10 + Wis ×0.62 + Int ×0.18 (80% of the tier's reference, on the target; 40% of [X] on every Soaked enemy). Also strikes every other **Soaked** enemy for 40% of [X] (new).
- **Description:** "Lash one enemy with the tide for [X] Water damage; the water carries on to every other Soaked enemy for 40% of that."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 67; Level 100: 273.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A payoff for a soaked field that still starts from one target.

### Mana Tide: Tier 4, defensive, all allies but you (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver. Every ally but you regains 5% of their maximum Mana (new).
- **Description:** "Raise a tide of Mana around your companions: every ally but you regains 5% of their maximum Mana."
- **Augments:** Mana cost.
- **Why:** Mana Spring for the whole party, small, and never for the caster, as Mana stays scarce (SP17).

### Swirling Eddy: Tier 4, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 10 + Wis ×0.56 + Int ×0.16 (60% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less its sure Soak). Always **Soaks** every enemy struck for 2 turns, and none of them can flee for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Catch all enemies in a swirling eddy for [X] Water damage: every one of them is Soaked and can't flee for 2 turns."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 85; Level 100: 247.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** An earlier sure group Soak than Abyssal Torrent's.

### Water Prison: Tier 5, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 75 Mana, 8,800 silver; power 15 + Wis ×0.85 + Int ×0.24 (70% of the tier's reference). The target is held in water: it can't act on its next turn, but takes 50% less damage until then (new); it is **Soaked** for 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Seal one enemy in a prison of water for [X] Water damage: it can't act on its next turn but takes half damage while held, and it is Soaked for 3 turns."
- **[X]:** Level 40: 163; Level 100: 374.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A sure way to take one enemy out of a turn, at the cost of not hurting it meanwhile.

### Wellspring: Tier 5, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 75 Mana, 8,800 silver. For 3 turns, every heal your side gives also restores Mana to the healer equal to 10% of the healing (new).
- **Description:** "Open a wellspring under the party: for 3 turns, every heal also returns a tenth of its healing to the healer as Mana."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Lets a healing party heal longer, as Mana stays scarce.

### Tsunami: Tier 6, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 4 AP, 120 Mana, 11,700 silver; power 22 + Wis ×1.32 + Int ×0.38 (90% of the tier's reference, the 4 AP rule on an all-enemies spell). A heavy 4 AP wave; every enemy struck is always **Soaked** for 2 turns, and Burning ones are Steamed (the reaction). Each enemy also has a 30% chance to be knocked **Off-Balance** (Wind's status, borrowed, SP61) for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Bring a tsunami down on all enemies for [X] Water damage, Soaking every one of them. Each also has a 30% chance to be knocked Off-Balance. This spell costs 4 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 50: 308; Level 100: 582.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Water's biggest group hit below Tier 10, for most of a turn.

### Ocean's Embrace: Tier 7, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 140 Mana, 17,500 silver; power 26 + Wis ×1.55 + Int ×0.44 (85% of the tier's reference). Heals [X] plus 30% of maximum HP; any healing beyond full HP becomes a **barrier** for 3 turns (new).
- **Description:** "Hold yourself or an ally in the ocean's embrace: [X] HP plus 30% of maximum HP restored, and whatever goes past full becomes a barrier for 3 turns."
- **[X]:** Level 60: 425; Level 100: 682.
- **Augments:** Power, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A great heal that never goes to waste.

### Drowning Depths: Tier 8, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 180 Mana, 25,000 silver; power 22 + Wis ×1.26 + Int ×0.36 (60% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less the AP loss). Every enemy that is **Soaked** is dragged down: it loses 1 AP at its next turn (new).
- **Description:** "Pull all enemies into the drowning depths for [X] Water damage; every Soaked one loses 1 AP on its next turn."
- **[X]:** Level 70: 399; Level 100: 556.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Undertow Spear's drag for a whole soaked field.

### Eternal Spring: Tier 9, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 250 Mana, 35,000 silver. For 3 turns, every ally regains 8% of their maximum HP and 4% of their maximum Mana at the start of each of their turns, and can't be set Burning (new).
- **Description:** "Let an eternal spring flow through the party for 3 turns: every ally regains 8% of maximum HP and 4% of maximum Mana at the start of each of their turns, and no fire takes hold."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Water's great sustaining spell, for HP and the scarce Mana alike.
