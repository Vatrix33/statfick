# New spells 4: Earth (task 12)

**Status: a draft for the user's review (SP2, SP3).** Earth's fourteen new spells: two in each of Tiers 1–5, one in
each of Tiers 6–9. They follow the approved system and curve (`Spell-Augmentation.md`), the sample's style and the
element statuses (`Element-Statuses.md`). Prices are the tier's usual. [X] values are for the raw caster of the system
draft (no classes or gear) at the level the tier unlocks and at Level 100.
**Updated 2026-11-12 (SP60):** some spells here borrow another element's status; each says so ("borrowed, SP60").

## What Earth gains

- **A tank's tools:** Stoneskin (an ally's guard at Tier 1), Earthen Wall (ranged attacks hurt less), Taunting Stone
  (a taunt: enemies' attacks drawn to one hero), Granite Bulwark (a barrier and Armor for the party).
- **Cracks, sooner and more:** Pebble Shot (2 AP), Sandblast (a group Crack at Tier 2), Crystal Shards (four rolls),
  Tectonic Spike (2 AP at Tier 8), Shattered Earth (every enemy to the full 3 stacks).
- **Heavy blows:** Boulder and Mudslide knock enemies down (Mudslide surely on Soaked ones), Earthquake (4 AP) shakes
  every enemy, Turn to Stone takes one enemy out of a turn and leaves it Cracked.
- **For fighters:** Seismic Charge gives an ally's weapon Earth damage and Cracks for 3 turns.

New mechanics: a taunt, ranged damage cut by a wall, petrification (a stun with a cooldown on the same enemy, half
resisted by bosses).

## The spells

### Pebble Shot: Tier 1, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 2 AP, 8 Mana, 700 silver; power 4 + Int ×0.23 + Str ×0.07 (65% of the tier's reference, the 2 AP rule). A 25% chance to **Crack** the target (1 stack) for 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Shoot a pebble at one enemy for [X] Earth damage, with a 25% chance to Crack it for 3 turns. This spell costs 2 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 7; Level 100: 94.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Earth's fast attack at Tier 1 (Knocked charges a weapon, Quick Drill is at Tier 2).

### Stoneskin: Tier 1, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 12 Mana, 700 silver. They take 15% less weapon damage for 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Harden the skin of yourself or an ally to stone: 15% less weapon damage for 3 turns."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Earth's first guard for an ally (Minor Fortify is for yourself).

### Sandblast: Tier 2, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver; power 6 + Int ×0.35 + Str ×0.1 (65% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less its effect). Each enemy struck has a 30% chance to be **Cracked** (1 stack) for 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Scour all enemies with blasting sand for [X] Earth damage; each has a 30% chance to be Cracked."
- **[X]:** Level 10: 23; Level 100: 142.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A group Crack a tier earlier than Faultline.

### Earthen Wall: Tier 2, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver. Ranged weapon attacks against your side deal 30% less for 3 turns (new).
- **Description:** "Raise an earthen wall before the party: for 3 turns, ranged attacks against them deal 30% less."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Wind's Deflect Missiles makes arrows miss; Earth's wall makes the ones that hit hurt less.

### Taunting Stone: Tier 3, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 34 Mana, 3,300 silver. For 2 turns, every enemy's single-target attack has a 50% chance to aim at them instead of its chosen target, and they take 15% less damage (new: a taunt).
- **Description:** "Mark yourself or an ally with a taunting stone for 2 turns: enemies' attacks are drawn to them half of the time, and they take 15% less damage."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Earth guards the party; a taunt lets its tank do so on purpose.

### Boulder: Tier 3, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 11 + Int ×0.65 + Str ×0.19 (85% of the tier's reference). A 40% chance to knock the target **Off-Balance** (Wind's status, borrowed, SP60) for 2 turns, ready for Earth's Topple, and a 30% chance to **Crack** it (1 stack) for 3 turns (the knock-down's AP loss becomes the Off-Balance).
- **Description:** "Roll a boulder into one enemy for [X] Earth damage, with a 40% chance to knock it Off-Balance and a 30% chance to Crack it."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 67; Level 100: 264.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A plain heavy blow for the early tiers.

### Mudslide: Tier 4, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 9 + Int ×0.52 + Str ×0.15 (55% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less its effect). Each enemy struck has a 30% chance to lose 1 AP at its next turn, a **Soaked** one for certain; then each has a 30% chance to be **Soaked** (Water's status, borrowed, SP60) for 2 turns (mud is earth and water).
- **Description:** "Bring a mudslide down on all enemies for [X] Earth damage; each has a 30% chance to lose 1 AP on its next turn (every Soaked one does), and then a 30% chance to be Soaked."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 73; Level 100: 211.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** An Earth spell for a party with Water in it.

### Crystal Shards: Tier 4, offensive, random enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 4 + Int ×0.24 + Str ×0.07 (26% of the tier's reference, per shard; four, 104% in all). Four shards (new) each strike a random enemy for [X], each with a 30% chance to **Crack** it (1 stack, 3 turns) and a 15% chance to make it **Bleed** (Blood's status, borrowed, SP60) for 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Hurl four crystal shards at random enemies, each dealing [X] Earth damage with a 30% chance to Crack and a 15% chance to make its target Bleed."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 34; Level 100: 97 (each shard).
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Several rolls stack Cracks, like Basic's Arcane Barrage stacks Exposed.

### Turn to Stone: Tier 5, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 75 Mana, 8,800 silver; power 12 + Int ×0.67 + Str ×0.19 (55% of the tier's reference). The target is turned to stone (new): it can't act on its next turn; when it wakes it is **Cracked** with 2 stacks for 3 turns. The same enemy can't be turned to stone again for 3 turns; a boss resists half the time.
- **Description:** "Turn one enemy to stone for [X] Earth damage: it can't act on its next turn and wakes Cracked twice over. It can't be petrified again for 3 turns, and a boss resists half the time."
- **[X]:** Level 40: 120; Level 100: 272.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Earth's sure way to take one enemy out of a turn, with a guard against chaining it.

### Seismic Charge: Tier 5, offensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **Description:** "Charge the weapon of yourself or an ally with seismic force for 3 turns: each attack deals [X] more Earth damage, with a 50% chance to Crack."

### Earthquake: Tier 6, offensive, all enemies (new spell)
