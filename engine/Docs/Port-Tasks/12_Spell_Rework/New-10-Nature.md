# New spells 10: Nature (task 12)

**Status: a draft for the user's review (SP2, SP3).** Nature's fourteen new spells: two in each of Tiers 1–5, one in
each of Tiers 6–9, among them **Nature's new summon** (the task gives Nature and Death one each). They follow the
approved system and curve (`Spell-Augmentation.md`), the sample's style and the element statuses
(`Element-Statuses.md`). Prices are the tier's usual. [X] values are for the raw caster of the system draft (no classes
or gear) at the level the tier unlocks and at Level 100, for a Wisdom-first caster.

## What Nature gains

- **Summon - Dryad** (Tier 4): a healer among Nature's beasts, made from the game's Dryad Matriarch (no new creature
  needed); as your summon, its Touch Grass heals your most wounded ally.
- **Rooting, cheaper and longer:** Seed Shot (2 AP), Grasping Weeds (no damage, every enemy), Strangling Vines (a
  sure 3-turn Root that chokes), Nature's Wrath (2 AP at Tier 6), Wrath of the Wilds (every enemy for 3 turns).
- **Growth over time:** Thorn Prick, Swarm (Nature's damage over time, its own and not Blood's Bleeding),
  Photosynthesis (HP and Mana each turn).
- **For the party:** Wildgrowth (a heal, and attackers may be Rooted), Gaia's Embrace (a heal that removes every
  debuff).
- **Wild power:** Spore Cloud (drowsy enemies lose AP), Stampede (4 AP, tramples every enemy), Bear's Form (a
  shapeshift: a bear's strength, but no spells).

New mechanics: shapeshifting (stats for spells), a summon whose heals go to the party, a swarm that blinds and bites
each turn, attackers rooted for striking an ally.

## The spells

### Seed Shot: Tier 1, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 2 AP, 8 Mana, 700 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.23 + Mind ×0.07 (65% of the tier's reference, the 2 AP rule). A 25% chance to **Root** the target for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Shoot a hard seed at one enemy for [X] Nature damage, with a 25% chance to Root it. This spell costs 2 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 7; Level 100: 102.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Nature's fast spell at Tier 1 (Thorns Shot is the Tier 2 one).

### Thorn Prick: Tier 1, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 12 Mana, 700 silver; power 4 + Wis ×0.24 + Mind ×0.07 (70% of the tier's reference, now; 25% of [X] again at each of its next 2 turns). The thorn grows (Thorn Tyrant's growth, small): at the start of each of its next 2 turns the target takes 25% of [X] again.
- **Description:** "Prick one enemy with a growing thorn for [X] Nature damage; it takes a quarter of that again at the start of each of its next 2 turns."
- **[X]:** Level 1: 7; Level 100: 106.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Nature's small damage over time, its own and not Blood's Bleeding.

### Grasping Weeds: Tier 2, offensive, all enemies (no damage) (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 14 Mana, 1,450 silver. Each enemy has a 30% chance to be **Rooted** for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Raise grasping weeds under all enemies; each has a 30% chance to be Rooted for 2 turns."
- **Augments:** Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A cheap way to Root a group before Briar Volley's damage.

### Photosynthesis: Tier 2, defensive, yourself or an ally (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 22 Mana, 1,450 silver. For 3 turns they regain 4% of their maximum HP and 2% of their maximum Mana at the start of each of their turns.
- **Description:** "Let yourself or an ally drink the light for 3 turns: 4% of maximum HP and 2% of maximum Mana return at the start of each of their turns."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** Steady growth for one hero, Mana included, as Mana stays scarce.

### Spore Cloud: Tier 3, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver; power 7 + Wis ×0.42 + Mind ×0.12 (55% of the tier's reference, 75% for all enemies, less its effect). Each enemy struck has a 30% chance to be drowsy: it loses 1 AP at its next turn.
- **Description:** "Release a cloud of drowsy spores over all enemies for [X] Nature damage; each has a 30% chance to lose 1 AP on its next turn."
- **[X]:** Level 20: 46; Level 100: 185.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A soft group slow in Nature's own way.

### Bear's Form: Tier 3, defensive and offensive, yourself (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 35 Mana, 3,300 silver. For 3 turns you take a bear's form (new): Strength and Vitality +25% and Armor +20%, but you can't cast spells (you can end it early as a free action).
- **Description:** "Take the form of a bear for 3 turns: your Strength and Vitality rise by 25% and your Armor by 20%, but you can't cast spells. You can return to your own form at any time."
- **Augments:** Effect strength, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A shapeshifting caster who wades in when the Mana runs low.

### Summon - Dryad: Tier 4, summon (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 56 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 16 + Wis ×0.94 + Mind ×0.27 (100% of the tier's reference, as a summon). Summons a Dryad (the game's Dryad Matriarch, Level 18, 181 HP today); its maximum HP rises by 200% of [X] and every attack, heal and spell it makes by 25% of [X] (SP29). It casts the Nature spells it knows as an enemy (Leaf Bolt, Thorns Shot, Return to Form, Touch Grass), but as your summon its Touch Grass heals your most wounded ally instead of itself (new), and it calls no beasts of its own; its hits have a 40% chance to **Root** their target for 2 turns. One at a time.
- **Description:** "Summon a friendly Dryad whose maximum HP rises by twice [X] and whose attacks, spells and heals grow by a quarter of [X]; she heals your most wounded ally, and her blows have a 40% chance to Root. Only one may be active at a time."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 142; Level 100: 414.
- **Augments:** Mana cost, Summon Vitality, Summon Might, Summon Speed.
- **Why:** Nature's new summon (the task): a healer among its beasts, made from a creature the game already has.

### Strangling Vines: Tier 4, offensive, one target (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 50 Mana, 6,000 silver; power 11 + Wis ×0.66 + Mind ×0.19 (70% of the tier's reference, now; 25% of [X] again at each of its next 3 turns). Always **Roots** the target for 3 turns, and the vines choke it for 25% of [X] at the start of each of its next 3 turns.
- **Description:** "Bind one enemy in strangling vines for [X] Nature damage: it is Rooted for 3 turns and choked for a quarter of that at the start of each of them."
- **[X]:** Level 30: 99; Level 100: 291.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect duration, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A sure, long Root that also wears its target down.

### Wildgrowth: Tier 5, defensive, all allies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 3 AP, 75 Mana, 8,800 silver; power 10 + Wis ×0.61 + Mind ×0.17 (50% of the tier's reference, per ally; 75% for all allies, less its effect). Heals every ally for [X]; for 3 turns, every enemy that strikes an ally has a 40% chance to be **Rooted** for 2 turns.
- **Description:** "Let wild growth spread through the party: every ally regains [X] HP, and for 3 turns any enemy that strikes them has a 40% chance to be Rooted."
- **[X]:** Level 40: 116; Level 100: 267.
- **Augments:** Power, Effect duration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
- **Why:** A party heal that also grips whoever comes near.

### Stampede: Tier 5, offensive, all enemies (new spell)

- **The spell:** 4 AP, 90 Mana, 8,800 silver; power 20 + Wis ×1.16 + Mind ×0.33 (95% of the tier's reference, the 4 AP rule on an all-enemies spell). A heavy 4 AP stampede; each enemy struck has a 40% chance to be trampled and lose 1 AP at its next turn.
- **Description:** "Call a stampede through all enemies for [X] Nature damage; each has a 40% chance to be trampled and lose 1 AP on its next turn. This spell costs 4 AP."
- **[X]:** Level 40: 223; Level 100: 511.
- **Augments:** Power, Resistance penetration, Effect chance, Mana cost.
