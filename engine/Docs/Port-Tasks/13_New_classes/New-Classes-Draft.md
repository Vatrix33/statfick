# Task 13, New Classes: the draft, every new class's identity, levels, requirements and rarity

**Approved by the user (2026-11-12, their notes applied: Limitbreaker red, Paragon's requirement; NC6–NC9) and added to
the end of task 10's draft, `../10_Class_Rework/Class-Rework-Draft.md` (NC19), which the port reads; this file stays as
the task's record.** The one draft of task 13 (NC4): all 79 new classes, each with its
identity, its number of levels, its requirements and its rarity (colour), the task's four questions in its order.
Nothing is built: like task 10's classes these are for the port (NC1, C23). Once approved they go to the end of task
10's two drafts in `../10_Class_Rework/` (NC5), and their features are then made level by level in batches, as task
10's were (C22). The decisions behind it are NC1 onward in `engine/Docs/Engine-Plan.md`, "Decisions for task 13, New
Classes"; the task is `engine/Docs/Port-Tasks/13_New_classes.txt`.

**How to read it**
- **Identity:** a short description of the class's playstyle, without its skills or passives and without specific
  stat gains (C15). Each was checked against the 175 classes in task 10's drafts and task 14's two (Dungeon Delver,
  Throwing Specialist), so that no two classes do the same thing.
- **Levels:** proposed, 3 to 6 (task 10's classes have 1 to 10; most 4 to 7).
- **Requirements:** in the kinds the game already uses where it can (a level, other classes, spells known, a stat, an
  item equipped, a count the game keeps). Where a class needs a count the game doesn't keep yet, it says "(a new
  count)"; the list of them is at the end. Every one was checked against a turn's 5 AP (C28). **A focus class builds
  on its path** (NC3): it needs a class on that path, not always the starting class; a pair class needs one on each of
  its two paths (or a class that is already both, such as the Spellblade); the classes without a focus need none.
- **Rarity** (no colour, blue, purple, gold, and red above gold: the Unreality's and, from this task, the
  Limitbreaker's): the harder or more exotic a class is to get, the rarer, and the stronger its features will be made.
  **The hooded man and red classes** (NC7, for the port): while the player is Level 99 or lower he never offers
  information on a red class; from Level 100 the red classes join his offers, the Limitbreaker too (his old rule
  leaving out every class that needs above Level 100 no longer leaves it out).
- **Your notes** from the review of 2026-11-12 are applied; each changed class shows its note word for word, and the
  questions they raised were asked (NC6–NC8 in `engine/Docs/Engine-Plan.md`).

**Totals:** 79 new classes: the task's 37 by focus (4 melee, 4 ranged, 4 spells, 4 from the rogue's path, 2 for each of
the six pairs, 9 without a focus) and 42 more (NC2). Players can then acquire 250 classes (171 of task 10's 175, as Raid
Boss, Beast, Great Beast and Monster can't be had, and these 79); with task 14's two, 252. Rarity of the 79: 51 without a
colour, 22 blue, 4 purple, 1 gold, 1 red (task 10's 170: 109, 42, 13, 5, and 1 red).

# The task's classes by focus (37)

## Melee (4)

They build on the warrior's path (Weaponmaster, Rearguard, Barbarian, Knight).

### Twinblade

6 levels · rarity no colour

- **Requires:** Level 20 · Weaponmaster 3 · Make 30 attacks with a weapon in each hand (a new count)
- **Identity:** Fights with a weapon in each hand: each hand's strike sets up the other, and the longer it keeps both
  moving, the faster the blows come.

### Avenger

5 levels · rarity no colour

- **Requires:** Level 20 · Rearguard 3 · Win 15 battles in which a companion fell (a new count)
- **Identity:** Fights for those beside it: every blow its allies take, and every ally that falls, makes it hit harder
  against the enemy that did it.

### Bladebound

6 levels · rarity blue

- **Requires:** Level 30 · Weaponmaster 5 · Defeat 300 enemies with one and the same weapon (a new count)
- **Identity:** Bound to one weapon for life: the weapon it has fought with longest grows stronger with it, and fights
  best in its hands.

### Crusher

5 levels · rarity no colour

- **Requires:** Level 15 · Barbarian 3 or Knight 3 · A two-handed melee weapon equipped
- **Identity:** Swings the heaviest weapons there are: each blow staggers the enemy and pushes its next turn further
  away.

5 levels · rarity no colour

### Deadeye

6 levels · rarity blue

- **Requires:** Level 30 · Sharpshooter 5 · Defeat 25 enemies with a single ranged attack each (a new count)

### Harrier

5 levels · rarity no colour

- **Requires:** Level 20 · Archer 5 · Scout 3
- **Identity:** Never lets the enemy settle: shots that slow, pin and unsettle, so the party moves first and the enemy
  never gets close.

### Glyphmage

# The new counts and requirement kinds

What the port would add for these requirements (the game keeps none of them yet):

- Counts: attacks with a weapon in each hand (Twinblade), battles won in which a companion fell (Avenger), enemies
  defeated with one weapon (Bladebound), bows and crossbows reforged (Fletcher), enemies defeated with a single ranged
  attack (Deadeye), element reactions set off (Catalyst), attacks with a dagger (Cutthroat), hits on enemies with a
  status or stunned (Opportunist), boat trips (Corsair), the highest level above yours of an enemy defeated (Prodigy),
  battles won while carrying an enemy's status (Steadfast), battles won in a row (Conqueror), falls in battles won
  (Martyr), the most silver held (Financier), different masterworks owned (Collector), elements whose spells hit you
  (Chameleon), potions drunk in battle (Drunken Master), guild Escort quests (Knight-Errant), Great Hunts completed
  (Huntmaster), kinds of bow attacked with (Bowmaster), Sparkling enemies defeated (Treasure Hunter), battles won
  against an enemy above your level (Daredevil).
- Kinds: stats within a quarter of the lowest (Equilibrist); no artifacts equipped (Ascetic); a group of enemies, the
  outlaws (Vigilante: Bandit, Highway Bandit, Bandit Archer, Highway Bandit Archer, Caravan Bandit, Bandit Captain,
  Dune Raider and any added later).
- An item: the Moonfang (Lycanthrope), a key item consumed on acquisition like the Blood Crystal.
- Counts the game already keeps, used as they are: different kinds of enemy defeated (as the Chronicler's), battles
  won at night (as the Nightwalker's), giants defeated (as the Titan's), healing items used in battle (as the Field
  Medic's), ingredients foraged, items sold, guild quests completed, companions, spells and elements known.
